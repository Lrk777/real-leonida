Is GTA 6's Florida exaggerated?

GTA 6 is set in Leonida, a parody of Florida. I matched each of its six regions to the real counties behind them and checked five kinds of chaos with government and open data: hurricanes, sinkholes, alligators, homicide and road deaths.

How this was built

  • Region → county matching follows press map guides (PC Gamer, GTABase, GTABoom). It is a judgement call: the game is not a 1:1 map, and Mount Kalaga (a mountain) has no real Florida equivalent, so its match is marked low confidence.
  • Hurricanes: NOAA HURDAT2 best tracks. A county is "hit" when a storm's track, interpolated hourly, passes within 60 nautical miles of the county's centre at hurricane strength (≥64 kt), 1950-2025. Landfall counts use HURDAT2 landfall records since 1851.
  • Sinkholes: Florida Geological Survey subsidence incident reports, placed in counties by their coordinates. Most reports are not field-verified, so this measures reported ground collapse, not confirmed sinkholes.
  • Alligators: iNaturalist research-grade observations via GBIF. Counting raw sightings would just measure where people are, so the layer is alligators per 10,000 wildlife observations of any species in that county.
  • Homicide and road deaths: County Health Rankings 2025 (CDC death records, multi-year averages). Small counties with too few deaths are suppressed by the source and shown in grey.
  • Home prices: Zillow Home Value Index (mid-tier homes). Shown for context, not part of the score.
  • Score: each layer is turned into a percentile among Florida's 67 counties, then averaged. Code, tests and data-quality checks: github.com/Lrk777/real-leonida.
